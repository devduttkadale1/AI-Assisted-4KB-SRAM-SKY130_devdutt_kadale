Task 5 integrated SRAM timing signoff

Timing method:
  Constraint-based signoff directly from generated OpenRAM Liberty.
  No OpenSTA/OpenROAD executable is installed on this machine.
  The logical 1024x32 wrapper contains only direct wires and spare tie-offs,
  therefore it adds no RTL logic delay and inherits macro timing constraints.

Limiting corner: SS
Maximum characterized safe period: 2.241000 ns
Maximum characterized safe frequency: 446.229362 MHz
Setup requirement: 0.103000 ns
Hold requirement: -0.056000 ns
Minimum pulse width: 1.120500 ns

Important:
  446.229 MHz is the maximum characterized Liberty limit, not a guard-banded operating recommendation.
  External controller signals must meet the SRAM setup/hold constraints at the macro pins.
